The 5 Essential Sections

Each feature is designed to serve a specific visitor need — from first-time browsers to committed donors.

Feature 01
💳
Donation Section

The most critical conversion point. A frictionless donation experience with preset amounts, transparent fund allocation, and multiple payment options turns passive visitors into active supporters.

Preset Tiers (₹100 / ₹500 / ₹1000) Custom Amount UPI + Card Payment One-time vs Monthly 80G Tax Receipt Fund Allocation Display
Why it matters
  • Directly funds all programs
  • Removes payment friction
  • Builds recurring donor base
  • Shows exactly where money goes
Best placement
  • Homepage hero area
  • Dedicated /donate page
  • Sticky header CTA button
  • End of every campaign card
Feature 02
🙋
Volunteer Sign-Up

People who want to give time, not money, need a clear path to join. A smart form with skill-matching connects the right people to the right programs.

Skill Selector Availability Picker City / Location Auto Email Confirmation
Feature 03
📊
Impact Showcase

Donors want proof. Animated counters, real stories, and visual data build credibility and demonstrate real-world impact in a compelling, shareable format.

Animated Stat Counters Before & After Stories Annual Report PDF India Reach Map
Feature 04
🚀
Campaigns & Programs

Active campaigns with live progress bars and deadlines create urgency and give every donor a specific cause to champion — driving targeted, motivated giving.

Progress Bar Days Remaining Share on WhatsApp Direct Campaign Donate
Feature 05
💬
Testimonials & Stories

Real voices from beneficiaries, volunteers, and donors are the most persuasive content an NGO can publish. They humanise the work and reduce first-visit hesitation.

Beneficiary Stories Donor Quotes Video Embeds Partner Logos

Implementation Guide

Building It Right

Practical recommendations for when the real site is developed — tools, tips, and quick wins.

💳
Payment Integration

Use Razorpay or Instamojo — both support UPI, cards, and net banking. Show exactly what each tier funds: "₹500 = school supplies for 1 child for a month."

🙋
Volunteer Onboarding

Embed a Google Form or Typeform. Send a warm auto-confirmation email within seconds and follow up personally within 48 hours — volunteers feel valued immediately.

📊
Live Impact Numbers

Use CountUp.js for scroll-triggered animated counters. Update stats every quarter and link to a downloadable Annual Report PDF for full financial transparency.

🚀
Campaign Sharing

Give every campaign its own URL for direct WhatsApp & Instagram sharing. A real-time fundraising bar showing live progress is the single highest-converting element.

💬
Authentic Storytelling

Collect 30–60 second video testimonials on field visits. Even a smartphone video with good natural light works. Video converts 2× better than text quotes alone.

📱
Mobile-First Always

70%+ of NGO traffic comes from mobile. Ensure donate buttons are thumb-friendly, pages load under 3 seconds, and forms are auto-fill compatible for fastest completion.
